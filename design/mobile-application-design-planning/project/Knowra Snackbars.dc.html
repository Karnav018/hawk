It leaves from the control that caused it
The bar begins on the pill's own centre line and one layer behind it, so its first 40px of travel is hidden by the pill — it is seen leaving from underneath rather than appearing above. The mic dims to 22% while it is out.
Width and travel on one spring, 950ms out
56 → 362px and 0 → −79px animate together on cubic-bezier(.34,1.46,.5,1), peaking at 368px / −84px before settling over 224ms — that overshoot is what makes it read as elastic rather than as a resize.
Content arrives after the shape settles
Label and action stay at zero opacity for the first 20% of the cycle and are fully up by 27%, once the shape has settled. Nothing squashes mid-stretch — the radius stays 999px the whole way.
Reverse is faster — 420ms, no overshoot
Retraction runs 420ms against the 950ms opening, easing in and swallowing back into the same button. Springing on the way out would read as a second, unrelated event. Hold is 3.3s between them.
