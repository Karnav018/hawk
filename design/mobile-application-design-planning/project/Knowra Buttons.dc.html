1 Knowra — button systems

The current button is a 14px-radius filled rectangle — it belongs to no particular family, which is probably why it reads as generic. Five replacements below, each with a different idea about where hierarchy comes from: shape (1a), weight (1b), surface (1c), depth (1d), structure (1e). Every set shows primary, secondary, quiet and destructive on both grounds.

1A Full pill

Hierarchy from shape: the app's nav bar and every circular control are already 999px, so a pill button is the only shape that doesn't introduce a second language. Quietest option, and the most obviously "ours".
