1 Hawk v3 — full flow

The whole product in one language: ink ground, 28px cards, white cards for the one thing that matters on a screen, a single accent used only for the primary action and live state, Martian Mono strictly for metadata. Nine lanes, read left to right in each: A onboarding, B library and upload, C search, D profile, pricing and payment, E plan limits (locked states, quota alerts, upgrade prompts), F home and search routes (voice-default home, chat-default home, nothing found), G upload routes (files, images, link, scan, speak, indexing queue), H viewer routes (audio, image), I account routes (billing, privacy, delete). Every route in the product is drawn. The Accent tweak themes the UI only — the orb art is fixed. Bottom nav is two tabs plus profile — Home, Library, You. Vault lives inside profile, never in the nav.
