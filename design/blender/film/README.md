# Hawk launch film (painted paper-cut, 60 s, one take, 16:9)

The whole film is one continuous paper world filmed in one unbroken camera move, with no cuts, at
1920×1080. The scenes and motion are the approved one-take cut; the surfaces follow the painted look of
`reference/enhanced_look_reference_v2.png`: gouache-textured paper in navy, cream, brand blue and orange.
It is generated by three scripts, so any beat can be changed and rebuilt in seconds.

`alt_restyled/` keeps an alternative, fully redesigned version of the scenes (lamps, plants, orbit cloud,
sunset panel, speech bubble, podium). It was not chosen; it is kept only for reference.

## Final cut

- **Video:** `hawk_launch_film_painted.mp4`, 1920×1080, 50 s, 24 fps. The previous black-and-white cut is
  `hawk_launch_film.mp4`.
- **File:** `hawk_film_previous_textured.blend`. It is a copy of the file behind the previous cut
  (`hawk_film_previous.blend`, kept untouched), edited in place:
  1. `add_texture.py` repaints every material in the gouache look and reference palette, turns key caption
     words blue, switches to 16:9 and lowers the captions.
  2. `edit_dive.py` sends the camera into the hawk's eye and through the pupil to black, then opens the screen
     like an eyelid onto the Hawk eye, which blinks open. It also gives all lettering clean paper with no
     shadows, so text stays sharp.
  3. The film plays 1.2× faster through Blender's time remap (`frame_map_old/new = 6/5`, frames 1-1200); the
     keyframes stay in the original 1440-frame timeline.
- **Stills:** `screens/` has one 16:9 still per screen; the earlier look is in `screens/previous_look/`.
- The scripts below (`hawk_film.py` etc.) regenerate the scene layout from scratch; the final cut was made by
  editing the copy, not by rebuilding.

## Portrait (9:16)

- **Video:** `hawk_launch_film_portrait.mp4`, 1080×1920, 50 s, 24 fps, rendered from `frames_portrait/`.
- **File:** `hawk_film_portrait.blend`. The same story, motion, texture, dive and speed as the 16:9 cut, with
  every scene re-laid out for a tall frame (a camera re-aim alone cut pieces off):
  - A: the memories float above and around the person's head; the archive is taller and narrower (5 back
    stacks, 4 mid shelves, 2 front shelves).
  - B: the sky, the hills and the hawk sit 4 units higher, above the taller archive.
  - C: the eye sits above the storage ellipse, and the category chips form a 2×2 grid inside it.
  - D: the filename strips are stacked.
  - E: the eye sits above the person and the shelf. The found photo settles above the person, and the three
    other finds stack in a column on the right.
  - END: the end card is centred over the person's scene.
- **Frame:** 6.2 units wide at the usual camera height (lens 81.3 mm). That is the same pixels per unit as
  16:9, so paper and lettering are the same size on screen. Captions sit in the lower quarter, and long ones
  wrap onto two lines.
- **Rebuild** (a few seconds, no rendering):

```
cd design/blender/film
B=/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender
$B -b --factory-startup --python portrait/hawk_film_portrait.py   # layout -> hawk_film_portrait.blend
$B -b hawk_film_portrait.blend --python add_texture.py            # paint (keeps 9:16 and the captions)
$B -b hawk_film_portrait.blend --python edit_dive.py              # dive + eyelids sized to the tall frame
$B -b hawk_film_portrait.blend --python-expr "import bpy; s=bpy.context.scene; \
  s.render.frame_map_old, s.render.frame_map_new, s.frame_end = 6, 5, 1200; bpy.ops.wm.save_mainfile()"
```

`portrait/` holds the layout script together with the `paperlib.py`/`puppets.py` it was built with. Those
make the plain paper materials that `add_texture.py` repaints. `add_texture.py` and `edit_dive.py` detect a
portrait build from the scene's `portrait` and `dive_*` properties. Landscape files have none, so they are
unaffected.

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `paperlib.py` | The gouache paper material, the palette, cut shapes, lettering with `*blue emphasis*`, the keyframe writer (`Anim`) |
| `puppets.py` | The eye, the split-pin person, the painted hawk, memory cards (landscape and Goa-sunset photos), shelves, thought bubble, captions |
| `hawk_film.py` | The world layout, the one camera path (`cam_path`), the guide eye (`guide`), the person (`person`), each region's beats |
| `hawk_film.blend` | The built film. Open it and press Space |

```
# rebuild after editing a script (2 s)
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b --factory-startup --python hawk_film.py
# render every frame to frames/ (~2-3 s a frame at 1920x1080)
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b hawk_film.blend -a
# check a few frames at half size first
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b hawk_film.blend --python-expr "import bpy; bpy.context.scene.render.resolution_percentage=50" -o /tmp/pv_#### -F PNG -f 100,400,900
```

## The world, and how the camera travels through it

```
                 ┌──────────── NIGHT SKY ────────────────────────────── SKY ─────┐
  y≈7            │ B: painted hawk over blue hills ─► dives into its eye         │  END: eye + "Hawk"
                 │    hawk bursts into paper, the eye is left ──┐                │     ▲ tilt up
                 │         ▲ tilt up                            │ eye glides     │     │
  y≈0  ──────────┼─ A: person + memories ─► archive unfolds     └─► C: storage ─► D: filenames ─► E: ask, find, montage, close
                 x=0                                               x=15.5        x=27           x=39.5
  ═══════════════ one floor strip runs under everything ═══════════════════════════════════════════════
```

From frame 453 the Hawk eye is the guide: it glides from region to region and the camera follows it. The
person walks into E during the pan. Captions ride on a "top glass" that moves with the camera.

## Look

- **Paper stock:** deep navy page, cream paper, brand blue `#59d3ff`, orange accents, warm wood shelves with
  coloured books, blue night hills, a warm moon.
- **Painted surface (`paper_mat`):** two layers of broad brush strokes (one lighter, one darker) laid in a
  different direction on every piece, blotchy colour, light dry-brush specks and dark paper grain, a fibre
  bump, matte finish. Flat cut shapes read as gouache on card.
- **Memories:** the lost ones are tracing paper with a perforated edge; the found ones are painted: a
  landscape photo, the Goa sunset photo, a cream document with a blue highlight, a navy voice card with a
  blue waveform, a whiteboard with a blue box.
- **Motion:** poses on twos with a boil; the camera, the travelling eye and the walker's path on ones.
- **Captions:** cream serif, key words cut from blue paper.
